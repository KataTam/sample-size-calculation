# Review a teaching material

Choose a small section or activity. Write as much or as little as helps explain your experience; you can leave prompts blank. No R installation is needed.

**Material reviewed:** Link, chapter/section or app activity.

**What were you trying to understand or do?**

**Where did you get stuck?** Point to a word, sentence, figure, code/output block or step. Describe what you expected it to mean or do.

**What helped you understand?** Give a specific example and explain why it helped.

**What would you change?** Suggest wording, a missing explanation, a different order or a useful example.

**What clinical question or example would make this more relevant to you?** Please avoid identifiable patient details.

**What are you still unsure about?**

**Anything else you would like the teacher to know?**

Send the completed notes to your teacher through the agreed course channel. Do not put names or sensitive information into a public issue. Participation does not require public sharing; permission and preferred attribution are discussed separately.
