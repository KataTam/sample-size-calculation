# Student case and assumption map

Draft a hypothetical clinical case or use a topic with public evidence. Do not include identifiable patient data. Label every invented value as a teaching assumption. The core comparison fields support equal independent groups with normal continuous outcomes or binary outcomes. The single-proportion extension supports descriptive/feasibility estimation; use a different validated method for other designs.

For a single-proportion estimation or pilot process-measure case, replace the two-group fields with the parameter, confidence level, useful margin of error, available total n and sampling plan. For an unsupported design, record the intended method and obtain design-specific advice before using the app.

For every evidence source, check its population, outcome definition, time point and analysis. Identify the least certain assumptions. A clinical threshold needs stakeholder justification; a small pilot's observed treatment difference is not a reliable substitute for that judgment. Choosing a target larger than the clinical threshold means accepting lower power at some worthwhile differences.

## Core interpretation and sensitivity

Interpret a prepared estimate and confidence interval against zero and the clinical threshold. Record plausible alternatives and their sources. Compare required sample sizes, then hold the selected sample fixed to assess how its performance changes. Include a table or curve with a written interpretation; simulation is not required for the core submission.

For a fixed existing dataset, describe the new question, eligible records, missing observations and attainable precision. For a pilot, specify each feasibility outcome's denominator and what findings would support proceeding, amending or stopping. Use the relevant information target rather than forcing either project into a treatment-effect calculation.

## Advanced extension: one study and repeated studies

Record the generating assumptions separately from the planning assumptions. Save the seed, replication count and analysis method, or attach the app export.

- Interpret one study's estimate, interval and p-value against zero and the clinical threshold.
- Report repeated-study rejection, coverage, mean width and probability of meeting the width target.
- State Monte Carlo uncertainty and explain why it differs from uncertainty in the clinical assumptions.
- Hold planned n fixed, change a generating assumption and explain the performance change.

## Justification and peer review

Write 300–600 words recommending proceeding, redesigning, changing the aim or not proceeding. Explain patient burden, informational value and limitations. If the goal is feasibility, say so explicitly. Ask a peer to identify one unsupported assumption and one conclusion that goes beyond the design.

Use the [assumptions report activity](https://katatam.github.io/sample-size-calculation/study/?activity=assumptions_report) to develop the same report while working through the tutorial. Select a two-group comparison or a single-population estimate, then the appropriate planning goal. Another design can be documented there, but its calculations need a matching method outside the basic apps.
