# Apps

Apps are interactive software. Activities provide questions and exercises to help you use them. The core apps accompany the [core tutorial](book/#core-route); the advanced options support further exploration.

<span id="core-calculations-simulation-and-justification"></span>

## Core apps

- [Study-plan report](assumptions_report/): develop a PICO question and justify the sample size, evidence and recruitment plan in a readable, reopenable report. [Activity: Develop and justify your study plan](study/?activity=assumptions_report).

- [Sample size reasoning lab](power_explorer/?activity=pain_one_many): simulate patient outcomes and compare results across studies. [Activity: Chronic pain: one study and many studies](study/?activity=pain_one_many).

- [Two-proportion calculator](two_proportions/): calculate sample size for a difference between two event percentages. [Tutorial explanation](book/calculations.html#two-proportions).

- [Two-mean calculator](two_means/): calculate sample size for a difference between two group means. [Tutorial explanation](book/calculations.html#two-means).

- [Recruitment adjustment](dropout_adjustment/): calculate recruitment allowing for expected losses. [Tutorial explanation](book/feasibility.html#account-for-expected-losses).

## Advanced apps and explorations

- [Sample size reasoning lab](power_explorer/?activity=pain_simulation): simulate how treatment estimates, confidence intervals, and detection probabilities change under different assumptions and missing-outcome scenarios. [Activity: Simulate repeated chronic-pain trials](study/?activity=pain_simulation).

- [Estimate a population percentage](prevalence_precision/): calculate sample size for a chosen confidence interval width, or assess the anticipated width for an available sample. [Activity: Estimate a percentage with useful precision](study/?activity=prevalence).

- [Type I and Type II error probabilities and power](sampling_distributions/): see false-positive and false-negative probabilities and power for a test of one population mean. [Activity: Explore error probabilities and power](study/?activity=alpha_beta).

- [Study-design questionnaire](design_questionnaire/): consider designs suited to your research question. [Activity: Choosing a study design](study/?activity=design_questionnaire).

The calculation apps use four views: “Overview” for the main result, “Explore assumptions” for graphs and comparisons, “Report” for a readable summary of current inputs and results, and “Methods and references” for calculation details. Reports download as PDF by default, with an editable Word alternative. The reasoning lab also has simulation and “My study” views; the study-plan report follows four writing stages. In the lab and study-plan form, separate “Save work for later” controls preserve settings and answers for reopening.

The reasoning lab has core and advanced activities. Core activities start with advanced controls closed; “Settings” provides the other assumptions. Question help is available by hover, keyboard focus or tap. Gray model answers in written-response fields illustrate how to explain a decision; reports use your own entries. The [activity walkthroughs](student-materials/activity-walkthroughs.html) provide guidance for each one, including pilot retention, precision and recruitment scenarios.

## Review and practice

- [Quizzes](quiz/): chapter checks and a cumulative quiz, with a Core or Core + advanced route. Results link to the relevant explanations; missed questions can be retried. Chapter checks are also embedded in the [tutorial](book/).
- [Flashcard preview](student-materials/flashcards/cards.md): a question-and-answer table with level filtering and search. [Anki and Quizlet downloads](student-materials/flashcards/index.md) are available for review outside the tutorial.

## Tutorial beside the app

Activity links open the tutorial and app together. You can drag the divider to adjust their widths, or use its Left and Right arrow keys. “Close app panel” returns to the tutorial at full width. “Tutorial + app” reopens the app with your current entries and results retained while the page stays open. On narrow screens, “Tutorial” and “App” switch between full-width panels.
