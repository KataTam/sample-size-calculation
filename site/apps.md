# Apps

Apps are interactive software. Activities provide questions and exercises to help you use them. The [four core activities](book/#core-route) use the apps below. The continuing report brings their results together; advanced options support further exploration.

<span id="core-calculations-simulation-and-justification"></span>

## Core apps

- [Study plan](assumptions_report/): develop a PICO question and justify the calculation and recruitment plan. The short core view and expanded advanced view use one set of answers, producing a concise sample size calculation report followed by the supporting study plan.

- [Sample size reasoning lab](power_explorer/?activity=pain_one_many): simulate patient outcomes and compare results across studies. [Activity: Chronic pain: one study and many studies](study/?activity=pain_one_many).

- [Two-proportion calculator](two_proportions/): calculate sample size for a difference between two event percentages. [Tutorial explanation](book/calculations.html#two-proportions).

- [Two-mean calculator](two_means/): calculate sample size for a difference between two group means. [Tutorial explanation](book/calculations.html#two-means).

- [Recruitment adjustment](study/?activity=recruitment): apply one expected loss percentage to the analyzable count from your chosen calculation. [Tutorial explanation](book/calculations.html#account-for-expected-losses).

The core [calculation activity](book/calculations.html#calculate-sample-size) uses the probiotics example. The [rehabilitation calculation](book/calculations.html#rehabilitation) provides optional practice with two means. The fourth core activity is the embedded [report review and completion](book/reporting.html#check-reporting).

## Advanced apps and explorations

- [Recruitment extensions](study/?activity=recruitment_advanced): examine unequal losses and treatment switching. [Tutorial explanation](book/simulation.html#recruitment-extensions).

- [Advanced study plan](assumptions_report/?level=advanced): appraise evidence, justify ranges and compare sensitivity scenarios in the same report.

- [Mean-difference estimation](power_explorer/?activity=mean_precision): calculate a sample size for a useful confidence-interval width and examine intervals across simulated studies. [Tutorial explanation](book/estimation-planning.html#plan-for-precision).

- [Sample size reasoning lab](power_explorer/?activity=pain_simulation): simulate how treatment estimates, confidence intervals, and detection probabilities change under different assumptions and missing-outcome scenarios. [Activity: Simulate repeated chronic-pain trials](study/?activity=pain_simulation).

- [Estimate a population percentage](prevalence_precision/): calculate sample size for a chosen confidence interval width, or assess the anticipated width for an available sample. [Activity: Estimate a percentage with useful precision](study/?activity=prevalence).

- [Type I and Type II error probabilities and power](sampling_distributions/): see false-positive and false-negative probabilities and power for a test of one population mean. [Activity: Explore error probabilities and power](study/?activity=alpha_beta).

- [Study-design questionnaire](design_questionnaire/): consider designs suited to your research question. [Activity: Choosing a study design](study/?activity=design_questionnaire).

Your [study plan](assumptions_report/?stage=question) develops through the core chapters. The Report view in each app offers “Add this calculation to my study plan.” A preview lets you attach the selected result and its inputs; your written answers remain in place. “Review before downloading” identifies unresolved decisions and checks attached calculation and recruitment counts.

The calculation apps use four views: “Overview” for the main result, “Explore assumptions” for graphs and comparisons, “Report” for a readable summary of current inputs and results, and “Methods and references” for calculation details. Reports download as PDF by default, with an editable Word alternative. The reasoning lab also has simulation and “My study” views; the study plan follows four writing stages. In the lab and study-plan form, separate “Save work for later” controls preserve settings and answers for reopening.

Each slider has an editable number beside it. Percentages use the displayed percentage scale: entering 5 means 5%. “Study settings” collapses in a narrow panel to leave room for the result. The introductory pain activity starts with its simulation, report and methods views; “Additional calculations and simulations” reveals the remaining views.

The reasoning lab has core and advanced activities. Core activities start with advanced controls closed; “Settings” provides the other assumptions. Question help is available by hover, keyboard focus or tap. Expandable “Example answer” sections illustrate a response using the current parameters. Reports use your own entries. The [activity walkthroughs](student-materials/activity-walkthroughs.html) provide guidance for each one, including pilot retention, precision and recruitment scenarios.

## Review and practice

- [Quizzes](quiz/): chapter checks and a cumulative quiz. Results link to the relevant explanations; missed questions can be retried. Chapters 1–2 and the advanced chapters have embedded quizzes. Chapter 3 uses the [four-error report review](book/reporting.html#check-reporting). The [discharge exercise](book/exercises.html#discharge) offers additional basic calculation practice.
- [Flashcard preview](student-materials/flashcards/cards.md): a question-and-answer table with level filtering, search and optional hidden answers for self-testing. [Anki and Quizlet downloads](student-materials/flashcards/index.md) are available for review outside the tutorial.

## Tutorial beside the app

Activity links open the tutorial and app together. You can drag the divider to adjust their widths, or use its Left and Right arrow keys. “Close app panel” returns to the tutorial at full width. “Tutorial + activity” reopens the app with your current entries and results retained while the page stays open. On narrow screens, “Tutorial” and “Activity” switch between full-width panels.
