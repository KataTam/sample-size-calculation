Develop and justify your study plan

Your study plan develops through the four core chapters. You can begin with your question, record decisions that need evidence, and return to complete the calculation and recruitment sections.

A saved plan is available in this browser. You can resume it or start a new plan. Editing the title or starting point also resumes your saved answers.

    Guidance behind the questions

    The calculation record also draws on section 4.1 of the UMCG Statistical Analysis Plan template (8A.35851, version 3): primary outcome, planned test, effect assumption, error probability, power, adjustments and software. UMCG students and researchers can access the SAP template in Zenya. The protocol, SAP and calculation should use consistent outcome definitions and methods, with changes documented.

    This educational planning worksheet draws on PICO (Richardson et al., 1995), selected SPIRIT 2025 protocol items on design, participants, interventions, outcomes, sample size and recruitment (items 12–16, 19–20), and DELTA² (Cook et al., 2018) on justifying the target difference and calculation assumptions. A full protocol should follow the complete guidance for its design.
