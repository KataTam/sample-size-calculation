Develop and justify your study plan

By the end of this activity, you will have a short study plan explaining your research question, sample size and the evidence behind your choices. You can use your own study or the hypothetical pain-relief case. The first section is enough to begin; later chapters help you complete the calculation and recruitment plan.

Help appears when you hover over, focus on or tap a question. Gray model answers illustrate what you could write. Only your own entries are included in the report.

    Guidance behind the questions

    This educational planning worksheet draws on PICO (Richardson et al., 1995), selected SPIRIT 2025 protocol items on design, participants, interventions, outcomes, sample size and recruitment (items 12–16, 19–20), and DELTA² (Cook et al., 2018) on justifying the target difference and calculation assumptions. A full protocol should follow the complete guidance for its design.
