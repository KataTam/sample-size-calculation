    Guidance behind the questions

    The calculation record also draws on section 4.1 of the UMCG Statistical Analysis Plan template (8A.35851, version 3): primary outcome, planned test, effect assumption, error probability, power, adjustments and software. UMCG students and researchers can access the SAP template in Zenya. The protocol, SAP and calculation should use consistent outcome definitions and methods, with changes documented.

    This educational planning worksheet draws on PICO (Richardson et al., 1995), selected SPIRIT 2025 protocol items on design, participants, interventions, outcomes, sample size and recruitment (items 12–16, 19–20), and DELTA² (Cook et al., 2018) on justifying the target difference and calculation assumptions. A full protocol should follow the complete guidance for its design. The complex-design record links the guidance for additional design and testing decisions.

    The evidence-review prompts also draw on the studies discussed in Choosing plausible assumptions.
