# Case: Chronic Pain Painkiller Study

This example compares a novel painkiller with standard treatment in patients with chronic pain.

## Outcome

Binary outcome: patient reports whether the painkiller was effective.

## Initial Assumptions

- Standard treatment event rate: 0.30
- Novel treatment event rate: 0.60
- Alpha: 0.05
- Power: 0.90
- Dropout: to be determined

## Teaching Use

Use this case to discuss how the smallest clinically relevant difference affects sample size and feasibility.


## Revised teaching specification

Treat the binary relief outcome as assessed at four weeks. Values 0.30 control and 0.60 treatment, a 0.30 absolute clinical threshold and all related assumptions are hypothetical teaching values. Separate the planning effect from the clinical threshold even when equal. Use the updated student case template and lab; compare generating treatment probability 0.40 at unchanged planned n. Do not use the tiny pilot to claim these probabilities are established clinical facts.
