# Writing style

Write as a teacher explaining sample size reasoning to medical students.

- Start with a concrete clinical question, then explain the calculation and its interpretation.
- Preserve the author's direct teaching voice and familiar wording where it remains accurate.
- Use ordinary verbs, short explanations and specific instructions: “Change power from 80% to 90%. What happens to the sample size?”
- Explain statistical terms when they first matter. Distinguish per-group, total and recruitment counts.
- Avoid promotional claims, vague educational slogans and unnecessary summaries.
- Keep pedagogical rationale in the teaching documentation, rather than interrupting every student explanation.
- Use real student contributions only with permission; do not invent quotations or testimonials.
