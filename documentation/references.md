# References

The single maintained bibliography is [module/references.bib](../module/references.bib). Sources are cited at their point of use in the learner module. The complete learner bibliography appears in the [online book](../book/references.html), with APA 7 author–year citations and an alphabetical reference list. The shared style is maintained in `module/apa.csl`. Educator sources are identified in the evidence and design rationale.
